# news-image-20261120

Sum up Friday 20 November 2026's world news in one image. A Node 22 program
(standard library only) copies a 1024x1024 newspaper front page — drawn in
this session — to `/output/entry.png`.

## The stories on the page

- **Kyiv under fire** — Russia hits Kyiv with 400+ drones and 110 missiles;
  bridges and the power grid struck. Two Russian missiles land in Poland,
  a NATO member.
- **Kerch Bridge hit** — Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge and frees 14
  settlements in the Kharkiv region.
- **Shenzhen summit** — Trump, Xi and Putin sign a joint peace declaration
  after their trilateral summit.
- **The Hague** — the ICJ rules Myanmar guilty of genocide against the
  Rohingya.
- **Markets** — Anthropic IPO: $81bn, the largest ever; shares open 33%
  above the offer price.
- **Tech outage** — Microsoft 365 down globally for 9+ hours; Outlook and
  Teams offline worldwide.
- **Energy** — the US Navy blockades Iranian shipping in Hormuz; a tanker
  ablaze; the IEA calls it the biggest energy supply shock in history.
- **Also today** — COP31 draft in Antalya drops fossil fuels as activists
  protest; US-China agree human oversight of military AI; Russian Tu-95
  bombers enter Taiwan's air defence zone; EU-UK summit in Brussels, Merz
  rejects a "new Yalta" for Europe.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

No network, no clock, no randomness: two runs write byte-identical PNGs
(129,063 bytes, 1024x1024, valid PNG).
